frequently asked questions

faq

answers to common questions about how the extension works,
what it can and can't do, and how to troubleshoot issues.

what does this extension actually do?

it saves, manages, verifies and switches between discord session tokens locally in your browser. you can group sessions by folder, add favorites and notes, check if tokens are still valid, copy them, or export a full json backup.

does it send anything to a server?

no. everything is stored in browser storage and never leaves your machine. there are no external servers, no telemetry, no analytics and no accounts.

how do i get my current session token?

open discord in a browser tab, click the extension icon, and your current session token will appear at the top of the popup. you can save it directly from there.

how does "switch session" work?

select a saved session in the popup and click "switch". the extension replaces the token in the active discord.com tab and reloads it. you'll be signed into that account immediately.

how do i verify a token?

in the session list, click the checkmark icon next to any saved session to verify it. you can also click "verify all" to check every saved token at once. verified tokens show a green indicator; expired ones show red.

how do i export or back up my sessions?

click the export button in the header. it downloads a json file with all your saved sessions — name, folder, favorite status, notes, token and verification info. you can re-import it later.

does it work with discord on the browser or the desktop app?

it works with discord running in your browser — chrome or firefox. it does not interact with the desktop app at all.

can other extensions or websites read my saved tokens?

no. the extension's storage is isolated to the extension itself. other extensions and web pages cannot access it.

what happens if i uninstall the extension?

all saved data will be lost. export your sessions before uninstalling.

is this against discord's terms of service?

this extension is a local, personal-use tool. it does not automate anything, does not interact with discord's api, and does not send requests on your behalf. it only manages session tokens that are already in your browser. use it at your own discretion.
