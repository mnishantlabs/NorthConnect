Frequently Asked Questions

How does YouTube & Spotify streaming work?

NorthConnect resolves the high-quality audio stream in real-time and pipes raw PCM audio (48kHz stereo, 16-bit) directly into Discord's voice gateways through FFmpeg memory pipes. Nothing is ever saved to your disk, ensuring near-instant playback with zero clutter.

Where are my Discord tokens saved?

All tokens and user settings live exclusively on your local machine in your Windows user AppData directory, encrypted at rest. The application contains zero telemetry and never transmits tokens to any third party.

Can I broadcast audio to a specific token or all tokens?

Yes! Use the Broadcast Target dropdown in the Player top bar to switch between broadcasting to All connected tokens or directing audio output to a single specific token.
